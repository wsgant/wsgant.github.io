Research

I work in motivic homotopy theory, using $\mathbb{A}^1$-homotopy-theoretic methods to attack questions in algebra and algebraic geometry. Most recently I have been studying algebraic vector bundles: when they split, when they admit nowhere-vanishing sections, and what obstructions control this.

My thesis studied when the canonical map between Stiefel varieties admits a section, a question that governs when a free summand can be split off a stably free module and which serves as an algebraic analogue of the classical problem of vector fields on spheres. I am also interested in algebraic topology more broadly.
